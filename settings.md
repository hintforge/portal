> Menu-tab names below are organized by the Source-engine Options menu's typical structure (Video / Audio / Keyboard / Mouse). Exact in-game tab labels were **not confirmed** by P1 sources -- treat the tab grouping as approximate and the per-setting effects as the verified content. Several puzzle-clarity settings live only behind console commands (see `controls.md`).

## Video

| Setting | Menu path (approx.) | Effect on play |
|---|---|---|
| Field of View (FOV) | Options → Video (default is narrow, ~75) | Raising FOV widens situational awareness -- helpful for fling awareness and spotting portal surfaces. In-menu slider is limited; widen further via `cl_fov` / `fov_desired` (requires `sv_cheats`, which disables achievements). |
| Motion blur | Options → Video | **Disable recommended** -- reduces visual clarity during fast movement / flings. |
| Vsync | Options → Video | Disable if you notice input lag during fast portal traversal. |
| Resolution | Options → Video | Higher resolution sharpens portal-edge clarity (helps edge placements / Camera Shy). |
