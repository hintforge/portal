Several puzzle-clarity settings live only behind console commands (see `controls.md`).

## Video

| Setting | Menu path (approx.) | Effect on play |
|---|---|---|
| Field of View (FOV) | Options → Video (default is narrow, ~75; the slider covers 75 to 110, and topped out at 90 before the 2022 patch) | Raising FOV widens situational awareness -- helpful for fling awareness and spotting portal surfaces. To go past the slider, `fov_desired` reaches 120. |
| Motion blur | Options → Video | **Disable recommended** -- reduces visual clarity during fast movement / flings. |
| Vsync | Options → Video | Disable if you notice input lag during fast portal traversal. |
| Resolution | Options → Video | Higher resolution sharpens portal-edge clarity (helps edge placements / Camera Shy). |

## Keyboard / Mouse

- Mouse sensitivity affects portal-aiming precision; see `controls.md`.
- Developer console is enabled from **Options → Keyboard → Advanced → Enable developer console**.

## Accessibility

Portal predates most modern accessibility menus; community workarounds run through console commands (FOV widening, crosshair/HUD toggles). No dedicated accessibility tab documented. The Audio settings do include subtitles and closed captions.
