Aggregated from every `missable: yes` overlay claim in the corpus -- the cross-cutting answer to "what am I about to lose if I leave this chamber?"

## Run-missable (lost permanently within a single playthrough)

| What | Where | Window / PoNR | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Security cameras (33)** → Camera Shy | Chambers 02,03,04,05,10,11,13,15,16,17,18,19 | Latest camera in **Chamber 19**. Must detach all 33 in **one no-load run** -- the counter resets on level skip, reload, death-restart, and at game end. No cameras after Chamber 19. | Per-camera locations in `items/collectibles.md`. Threshold-check: leaving Chamber 02 you should read 3; if a count is ever low, restart. |
| **Friendly Fire** achievement | Turret chambers 16 and 18 | **Chamber 18** is the last turret chamber (PoNR). Not strictly missable while turrets exist, but gone once you leave Chamber 18. | Pick up one turret and physically knock over another with it. See `achievements.md`. |

## Not run-missable but easy to lose locally

| What | Where | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Radio in **Chamber 05** | Chamber 05 | **Pull it through a portal -- do NOT walk it through the exit door**, or you lose access to it. |
| Radios over goo/acid | Chambers 15, 18 | Quicksave before flinging a radio across goo; losing one can force a chamber replay. |

## Post-game gated (not missable, but require a prior completion)

| What | Where | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Hidden radios (26)** → Transmission Received | Chambers 00-19 + escape | Radios are present from a first run but only grant achievement progress **after the game is completed once**. Collect on a dedicated post-completion run. Locations in `items/collectibles.md`. |

## Common-knowledge correction

The widely repeated rule that *interrupting GLaDOS's camera voice lines breaks Camera Shy* is **false** -- the camera-detach counter and the voice-line counter are separate; only the detach counter feeds the achievement. _source: deep-research handoff 2026-06-02 · capture: web_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: easter-egg · spoiler: none_
