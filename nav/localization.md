# Portal -- Localization Toolkit

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02
**research_run:** P2 ingested 2026-06-02 (portal_p2.result.md)

Portal uses **`landmark` localization** (no map UI). The persona infers the player's position from what they describe seeing. The single most reliable cue is the **chamber number sign** at each entry antechamber (Aperture displays the chamber number and the featured testing-element icons).

**Cue hierarchy (most → least reliable):**
1. Chamber number sign at entry antechamber
2. Distinctive equipment (see per-chamber list below)
3. Architecture (platform count, grill placement, observation window)
4. GLaDOS audio (chamber-specific dialogue — quote the first distinguishing line)

## Per-chamber landmarks

### chamber_00 — Relaxation Vault & First Button

1. The **glass-walled Relaxation Vault** with a bed and table-mounted radio — UNIQUE; only chamber containing a living-quarters vault.
2. No portal gun in the player's hands.
3. Cube dispenser + single large round button, no portal surfaces used. Disambiguation vs. chamber_19's similar test room: chamber_00 has the vault and no portal gun.

GLaDOS: "Hello and, again, welcome to the Aperture Science Computer-Aided Enrichment Center." `[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_01 — Cycling Auto-Portal Rooms

1. Three sealed side rooms (cube / button / exit) around a central pit, accessed only as a **single static portal cycles** between them.
2. No portal gun.
3. Radio atop a unit beside the entry. Disambiguation: the cycling-single-portal mechanic is unique to chamber_01 (chamber_02 has a rotating gun on a stand instead).

GLaDOS: "Please place the Weighted Storage Cube on the 1500 Megawatt Aperture Science Heavy Duty Super Colliding Super Button." `[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_02 — Blue Portal Gun Acquisition

1. A **portal gun rotating on a central pedestal**, auto-firing portals — UNIQUE acquisition scene.
2. Entry observation window overlooking the whole room.
3. A wall section with a hole (radio location). Disambiguation: chamber_02 grants the blue-only gun; chamber_11 grants the dual gun amid toxic goo.

GLaDOS narrates the device pickup. `[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_03 — Three Piers

1. **Three piers/platforms over a gap** with a permanent orange portal on the MIDDLE pier — UNIQUE three-pier layout.
2. No cubes, buttons, or doors at all.
3. Camera directly beside the chamber-number sign. Disambiguation: "three piers, no cubes" distinguishes it from any cube chamber.

`[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_04 — Pit Cube & Single Button

1. A single cube dispenser dropping a cube into a pit with a permanent orange portal at the pit floor.
2. **Single floor button** (ONE button/ONE cube). Disambiguation: ONE button/one cube vs. chamber_05's TWO buttons/TWO cubes.
3. Two cameras (above the button; beside the exit).

GLaDOS: "we will not monitor the next test chamber." `[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_05 — Two Buttons, Two Cubes

1. **TWO floor buttons and TWO cubes** (one in a pit, one on a raised platform).
2. A permanent orange portal on one raised platform + a **fixed orange portal in the CEILING** above the chamberlock.
3. Three cameras. Disambiguation: two-button/two-cube + ceiling exit portal is unique vs. chamber_04.

GLaDOS: prior no-monitoring claim "was an outright fabrication." `[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_06 — First High Energy Pellet

1. First appearance of **brown (non-portalable) vs. grey (portalable) wall texturing**.
2. A **High Energy Pellet launcher + receiver** with a victory lift — the first pellet chamber.
3. Radio on the launcher; no cameras. Disambiguation: simplest single-pellet→lift layout; chamber_07/08 add moving platforms.

GLaDOS: the pellet "can and has caused permanent disabilities, such as vaporization." `[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_07 — Pellet-Powered Moving Platform

1. A pellet receiver that powers a **MOVING PLATFORM** (not a fixed lift).
2. Entry platform with radio hidden beneath the stairs.
3. Non-portalable surfaces constrain routing; no cameras. Disambiguation vs. chamber_06 (fixed lift) and chamber_08 (acid floor): chamber_07 has no lethal floor.

`[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_08 — Pellet Bounce over Acid Floor

1. A **lethal acid/goo floor** (first "death on floor contact" chamber).
2. Pellet must bounce off a wall and be reoriented before reaching the receiver.
3. Moving platform to the exit; radio under its start position; no cameras. Disambiguation: acid floor + pellet-bounce.

GLaDOS: "we have added a consequence for failure... followed by death." `[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_09 — Extreme Pessimism (Impossible Test)

1. An **emancipation grid dividing the room mid-puzzle** (cube can be destroyed/re-dispensed).
2. Radio wedged in the narrow gap between sections.
3. Cube + single button; no cameras. Disambiguation: the "impossible test" pessimism + mid-room grid.

GLaDOS: "this next test is impossible. Make no attempt to solve it." `[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_10 — Momentum (Speedy Thing Goes In)

1. **Floor diagrams illustrating momentum** (downward→horizontal), with fixed orange portals on suspended panels.
2. Multi-room momentum/fling progression with pits.
3. A SINGLE camera in the first room's upper corner. Disambiguation: floor momentum diagrams are unique to chamber_10.

GLaDOS: "speedy thing goes in, speedy thing comes out" / "how a portal affects forward momentum." `[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_11 — Dual Portal Gun Acquisition

1. The **dual portal gun on a central pedestal** reached by a pellet-powered moving platform.
2. A **toxic-goo main floor** (GLaDOS warns it "will kill you").
3. Radio above the entranceway; one camera. Disambiguation: dual-gun acquisition + goo floor (vs. chamber_02's blue-only dry room).

GLaDOS: device "now more valuable than the organs and combined incomes of everyone in `<subject hometown here>`." `[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_12 — Vertical Climb (Numbered Levels)

1. A **tall vertical shaft with platforms on ALTERNATING sides**, each marked by dot-count symbols.
2. A momentum pit at the bottom; cube dispenser feeding a 3rd-level button / 4th-level door.
3. Radio on a central platform support; no cameras. Disambiguation: the vertical dot-numbered climb is unique.

GLaDOS (fragmented): "…fling yourself…need to fling into space…" `[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_13 — Two-Step Buttons & Pellet Platform

1. A **two-step double-button puzzle** (both buttons normally inaccessible) with a pellet-powered platform and a one-dot ceiling symbol.
2. Three cameras (entranceway; main-room wall; final passage).
3. Radio in the final passageway. Disambiguation: first post-dual-gun puzzle; first Challenge chamber.

GLaDOS: "this next test could take a very, very long time." `[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_14 — Long Pellet Routing & Sludge Platforms

1. **Rising/retracting stairs** and a raised cube platform requiring a fling.
2. A **sludge room with platforms that rise and sink**, plus a pellet launcher antechamber and a long around-corners pellet route.
3. Radio on the first-room extended-platform support; no cameras. Disambiguation: rising-then-retracting stairs + long pellet routing.

GLaDOS: "A complimentary victory lift has been activated." `[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_15 — Double-Fling & Emancipation Grids

1. **Double-fling onto a checkered floor** through a force field; emancipation fields used MID-puzzle (not just at exits).
2. Twin side-rooms with buttons plus a pellet receiver controlling the exit lift.
3. **FIVE cameras and TWO radios**. Disambiguation: emancipation grids as in-puzzle hazards + two radios.

`[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_16 — Turret Introduction

1. **Sentry TURRETS** (first appearance) behind bullet-proof glass and in a button room marked with red X's.
2. A malfunctioning cube dispenser side room supplying shields.
3. Five cameras + fenced exit turrets. Disambiguation: turrets + "live-fire course" framing; the turret-dense layout is the Friendly Fire spot.

GLaDOS: "a live-fire course designed for military androids." `[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_17 — Weighted Companion Cube

1. The **Weighted Companion Cube** (heart-marked) accompanying the player.
2. Two pellet corridors crossed using the cube as a shield; a three-platform/three-receiver area.
3. The **Emergency Intelligence Incinerator** at the end + a Rattman den behind an extended panel. Disambiguation: heart-cube + incinerator is unique.

GLaDOS: "The vital apparatus vent will deliver a weighted companion cube in 3…2…1." `[Confirmed: 3]`

---

### chamber_18 — Three-Part Gauntlet

1. A **three-part gauntlet**: dot-symbol fling platforms, a turret+pellet room with a moving platform, then linked flings.
2. The second Rattman den.
3. Two cameras and **THREE radios**. Disambiguation: only chamber with three distinct parts and three radios; hardest standard chamber.

GLaDOS: "you will be baked, and then there will be cake." `[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### chamber_19 — The Final Test & Fire Pit

1. A familiar test-room layout with a pellet launcher, receiver, and two 45°-angled platforms, plus a **permanent GREEN pellet**.
2. An equipment recovery annex with a small button + a malfunctioning chamberlock.
3. The **fire pit / "party escort" oven** at the route's end. Disambiguation: green permanent pellet + fire pit + cake framing.

GLaDOS: "Welcome to the final test... you have been promised cake." `[Confirmed: 2]`

---

### escape_seq — The Escape & GLaDOS Boss

1. **Rusty maintenance tunnels** with Rattman writings and red directional arrows (no clean white test panels).
2. Piston rooms (five-piston room; ceiling-striking pistons), cube-transport tubes, and a turret AMBUSH room (three doors).
3. The **non-lethal green-generator goo** and GLaDOS's chamber with the rocket turret and incinerator. Disambiguation: broken industrial environment + Rattman graffiti distinguishes the escape from any clean chamber.

GLaDOS (taunting): "What are you doing? You haven't escaped, you know." `[Confirmed: 2]`

## Localization prompts

When `player_position.confidence` < `high` in CHECKPOINT.md, ask these in order (stop when position is confirmed):

1. "What's the last GLaDOS announcement you heard, word-for-word if you can?"
2. "Can you see a chamber-number sign at the entrance? What number does it show?"
3. "Do you have a portal gun in hand — and can you make BOTH orange and blue portals, only blue, or none yet?" (None → chambers 00–01; blue-only → chambers 02–10; both → chambers 11+.)
4. "Can you see a button platform? How many cubes or buttons are near it?" (One → chamber_04; two → chamber_05.)
5. "Is there a High Energy Pellet (a bouncing energy ball) with a launcher/receiver visible? Is the floor below it solid or filled with goo/acid?" (Goo/acid floor → chamber_08 or 11.)
6. "Do you see turrets? Are they behind glass, marked with a red X, or behind fencing?" (Turrets → chamber_16 or 18, or the escape ambush.)
7. "Are you carrying a cube with hearts on its sides, and do you see an incinerator?" (→ chamber_17.)
8. "Is the environment clean white panels, or rusty broken tunnels with graffiti and red arrows?" (Rusty/graffiti → escape_seq.)
