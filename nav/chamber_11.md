## Entry

From `chamber_10` via chamberlock elevator. Autosave on inter-chamber elevator transition (within `testchmb_a_05`, shared with chamber_10).

## Exit

To `chamber_12` via chamberlock elevator (one-way, chapter-bound). Dual-portal capability unlocked here.

## Outgoing edges

- `(chamber_11, chamber_12)` -- story-gate (elevator + grill), dual-portal ASHPD acquired here

## Entry tips

1. **One camera** (above the entranceway) and **one radio** (above the entrance, turn around on entry) — collectible before dropping onto the goo floor.
2. The floor is **toxic goo** — stay on raised surfaces and platforms.
3. Dual portal gun is acquired at the central pedestal (gate-05) — from this point you can fire BOTH orange and blue portals independently.
