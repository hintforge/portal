## Entry tips

1. **Five cameras** and **two radios** — the highest collectible density in the early/mid game. Take note of all locations listed in gate-06 and gates-02/04.
2. Emancipation grids appear MID-puzzle (not just at the exit) — plan portal placement before passing through them.
3. Three-room structure: double-fling entry room → exit-lift corridor → fling side room leading to two side-room buttons + pellet receiver.
