## Exit

To `chamber_18` via chamberlock elevator — but **the exit is locked until the Companion Cube is incinerated** (permanent within the run; Fratricide achievement).

## Outgoing edges

- `(chamber_17, chamber_18)` -- story-gate (elevator, post-Incinerator); key = Companion Cube incinerated; permanent lock within run

## Entry tips

1. **One radio** (beneath the third platform after it raises) — collect after gate-04 raises the platforms.
2. **Two detachable cameras**: one next to where the second pellet hits the wall, one above the doorway to the small pellet-launcher room. Other cameras in the chamber sit on walls that take no portals and do not count toward Camera Shy.
3. The Companion Cube is a tool — use it as a step and as a pellet shield in the corridors. The exit does not open until it is incinerated at gate-08.
