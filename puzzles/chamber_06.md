# Puzzle -- Chamber 06 (High Energy Pellet)

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-06-02
**research_run:** P1 ingested 2026-06-02

**Puzzle type:** Energy-pellet routing. First chamber to introduce the High Energy Pellet, its launcher and receiver, and non-portalable brown walls.

**Lvl 1 nudge:** This chamber introduces the bouncing energy pellet -- you need to get it from its launcher into the receiver to power the lift. Notice the red light the receiver shines on the ceiling.

**Lvl 2 hint:** The receiver's red ceiling light marks where a portal needs to go so the pellet's path lands in the receiver. Watch which walls are dark brown -- those won't accept a portal.

**Lvl 3 solution:** Place a portal so the pellet exits aimed at the receiver, using the red ceiling light as your aiming guide. The pellet kills in one hit, so keep clear of its path. (Pellets recharge their lifespan each time they pass through a portal, extending the window before they disintegrate.)
