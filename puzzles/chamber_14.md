# Puzzle -- Chamber 14

**status:** research-integrated
**last_reconciled:** 2026-11-06
**research_run:** P1 ingested 2026-06-02

**Puzzle type:** Fling / momentum. A challenge chamber combining cubes, pellets, vertical scaffolds, and goo.

**Lvl 1 nudge:** This chamber looks like it wants you to traverse several rooms -- but there's a faster route straight up out of the first room.

**Lvl 2 hint:** The intended long route works, but a single fling from the pit left by the retracted stairs can land you straight on the raised Victory Lift.

**Lvl 3 solution:** Without leaving the first room, put a portal on the ground in front of the Victory Lift, as close to it as you can, and the other at the bottom of the pit left by the retracted stairs. Jump in and steer your flight to land on the raised lift -- finishing the chamber early.
